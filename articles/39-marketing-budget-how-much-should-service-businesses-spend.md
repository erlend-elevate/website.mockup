# Marketing Budget: How Much Should Service Businesses Spend?

The question "how much should we spend on marketing?" is one of the most common — and most poorly answered — in business. Generic rules like "spend 10% of revenue" ignore your growth stage, competition, margins, and goals. This guide provides practical frameworks for setting a **marketing budget service business** that matches your specific situation.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **Start with goals, not percentages.** Work backwards from revenue targets.
> - **10% of revenue is a baseline** for established businesses; 15–25% for growth-stage businesses.
> - **New businesses should spend more** — you are building awareness from zero.
> - **Allocate by channel based on your funnel stage** — not equally across all channels.
> - **Review and reallocate quarterly** based on performance data.

## Budget Frameworks

### 1. Goal-Based Budgeting (Recommended)

Work backwards from your revenue target.

| Step | Calculation | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| Revenue target | €500,000 |
| Avg. client value | €5,000 |
| Clients needed | 100 |
| Close rate | 20% |
| Leads needed | 500 |
| Cost per lead | €80 |
| **Marketing budget** | **€40,000** |
| As % of revenue | 8% |

### 2. Percentage of Revenue

| Business Stage | Revenue Range | Marketing % | Annual Budget |
|---------------|--------------|-------------|---------------|
| Start-up | €0–€500K | 20–30% | €0–€150K |
| Growth | €500K–€2M | 12–20% | €60K–€400K |
| Established | €2M–€10M | 8–12% | €160K–€1.2M |
| Mature | €10M+ | 5–8% | €500K+ |

### 3. Competitor-Based

Estimate competitor spend using tools like SEMrush and Facebook Ad Library. Match or exceed spend in your most important channels.

## EU Market Considerations

- **Currency:** Budget in EUR; account for cross-border cost variations
- **GDPR compliance:** Allocate €500–€2,000/year for consent management and legal review
- **Multi-market:** Add 20–30% for localisation if targeting multiple EU countries
- **Seasonality:** Adjust budget seasonally (B2B slower in summer, retail peaks in Q4)

## FAQ

### Is 10% of revenue enough for marketing?
For established businesses with stable demand, yes. For growth-stage businesses or new market entrants, 15–25% is more realistic.

### Should I cut marketing spend during slow periods?
No. Cutting marketing during slow periods creates a death spiral. Maintain base spend and shift focus to retention and nurture rather than acquisition.

### How do I know if my marketing budget is working?
Track cost per lead by channel, lead-to-customer conversion rate, and overall marketing ROI. If CPL is stable and ROI is positive, your budget is working.

### What is the minimum viable marketing budget?
For a service business, €1,500–€2,000 per month is the minimum for meaningful data and results. Below this, you cannot gather enough data to optimise.

### How often should I review my marketing budget?
Monthly for active campaigns. Quarterly for strategic reallocation. Annually for comprehensive planning.
