## Why Google Ads for Home Services Works Differently Than Other Industries

Home services advertising operates on different logic than e-commerce or SaaS. A homeowner with a burst pipe does not compare ten options. They pick the first credible business that answers the phone. This changes how you build campaigns.

**The decision cycle is minutes, not days.** According to WordStream's 2024 industry benchmarks, the home improvement category averages a cost per click of approximately €6.40, with conversion rates around 6-8% for well-structured campaigns. That sounds expensive until you realise a single €400 boiler repair job covers 60+ clicks.

The high-intent nature of home services searches means broad match keywords are dangerous. "Plumbing" could mean a DIYer looking for a tutorial. "Plumber near me open now" means a booking. Your keyword strategy must filter out researchers and capture only the buyers.

Here's how to do it: structure campaigns around service intent, not service category. Separate emergency calls from planned installations. An emergency leak repair commands a higher bid than a bathroom renovation quote, because the homeowner books immediately. We saw this in a campaign for an electrical contractor in Munich — separating emergency keywords into their own ad group dropped cost per lead by 34% in the first month.

## Search Ads vs. Local Services Ads — Which Should You Run First?

Google offers two distinct advertising products for trades businesses. Understanding the difference saves you from spending money on the wrong system.

**Google Search Ads** are the traditional pay-per-click text ads that appear above and below organic results. You choose keywords, write ads, set bids, and pay when someone clicks. You control landing pages, ad copy, and targeting.

**Local Services Ads (LSAs)** are Google's pay-per-lead product. They appear at the very top of search results — above even regular paid ads. You do not pay for clicks. You pay only when a potential customer calls or messages you through the ad. Google handles keyword targeting automatically based on the services you select in your profile.

The trade-off is control. With LSAs, Google writes the ad format, displays your review score, and shows the "Google Guaranteed" badge after you pass their [verification process](https://support.google.com/localservices). That verification includes background checks, license confirmation, and insurance validation. It takes 3-4 weeks on average, but the badge converts at a higher rate because homeowners trust it.

| Factor | Google Search Ads | Local Services Ads |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Pricing model | Pay-per-click (PPC) | Pay-per-lead |
| Ad placement | Top/bottom of search results | Above all other results |
| Keyword control | Full control | Google matches automatically |
| Verification required | No | Yes — license, insurance, background checks |
| Trust signals | Extensions only | Google Guaranteed / Google Screened badge |
| Lead cost (typical EU) | €8-€25 per conversion | €15-€50 per lead |
| Best for | Immediate lead generation while building LSA profile | Established businesses with reviews and staff |

**Our recommendation:** Run Search Ads immediately while you start the LSA verification process. LSAs take weeks to approve, and you cannot afford to wait. Once your LSA profile is live and you have 5+ reviews, allocate budget to both. The two formats together typically capture 40-60% of above-the-fold real estate on high-value searches.

## Call-Only Campaigns: Getting the Phone to Ring

For trades businesses, a phone call is worth more than a website visit. Someone who calls is in active booking mode. Someone who clicks to your website might be comparing prices for the next three hours.

**Google call-only ads display your phone number as the headline with a prominent "Call" button.** They show exclusively on mobile devices. When a user taps the button, their phone app opens and dials you directly. No website visit. No form fill. Just a live conversation.

The key mistake 8 out of 10 service businesses make: they run call-only ads 24/7. If your office closes at 6 PM and you do not offer emergency service, a call that goes to voicemail is wasted money. Schedule call-only ads strictly within your answering hours. If you offer emergency service, run a separate emergency call-only campaign with higher bids for out-of-hours targeting.

Set your call conversion tracking to count only calls lasting 30 seconds or longer. A 5-second hang-up is not a lead. In our experience, calls over 60 seconds convert to booked jobs at roughly 3x the rate of shorter calls. Google Ads lets you define this threshold in your conversion settings — use it.

Use call extensions on all standard Search ads too. Research shows that ads with prominent phone numbers increase click-to-call rates by up to 250%. Make your phone number visible, use callout extensions like "Free Quote" or "Same-Day Service," and write ad copy that pre-qualifies the caller.

## Local Targeting That Does Not Waste a Single Euro

The fastest way to burn budget is showing ads to people outside your service area. A homeowner 40 kilometres away does not care that you are the best plumber in the city centre — they need someone local.

**Set your location targeting to "Presence" — not "Presence or interest."** The default "Presence or interest" setting shows your ad to people who have shown interest in your area, even if they are currently 500 kilometres away. For a plumbing business, that is worthless. "Presence" means the person is physically located in your target zone when they search.

Use radius targeting around your depot or primary service hub, not city boundaries. A 15-20 kilometre radius is typical for urban trades businesses. In rural areas, extend to 30-40 kilometres. Monitor your "where your users were" report in Google Ads weekly. If you are getting clicks from postcodes you cannot reach, exclude them immediately.

Layer location bid adjustments based on performance data. If postcodes within 5 kilometres of your office convert at twice the rate of outer areas, increase bids by 20-30% for that radius. Google Ads location reports show conversion rates by postcode — use this data ruthlessly.

For businesses operating across multiple towns, we recommend separate campaigns per major location. Separate campaigns let you write location-specific ad copy ("Plumbers in Stuttgart" vs. "Plumbers in Esslingen") and track cost per lead by town. One heating contractor operating across three German cities reduced cost per lead by 28% after splitting a single campaign into three location-specific ones.

## Seasonality and How to Plan Your Google Ads Calendar

Home services demand is not flat. HVAC companies see 3-4x search volume spikes during cold snaps and heatwaves. Plumbers face burst pipe emergencies in winter. Landscaping businesses peak in March-May and slow in November-February. Cleaning services see spikes before holidays and in January.

**The data shows that advertisers who maintain presence year-round, even at reduced spend, outperform those who pause completely in off-seasons.** When you pause campaigns, Google's learning algorithm resets. Resuming in peak season means 2-3 weeks of re-learning before performance recovers.

Here is a seasonal budget framework for common trades:

| Trade | Peak Months | Off-Peak Months | Recommended Strategy |
|-------|------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| HVAC / Heating | November–February | May–September | Increase budget 40-60% in peak. Shift summer budget to cooling/installation keywords. |
| Plumbing | Year-round (winter spike) | August–September | Maintain base budget year-round. Increase 25% during cold snaps. |
| Electrical | Year-round | December (partial) | Steady spend. Slight increase for outdoor lighting before Christmas. |
| Landscaping | March–May, September | November–February | Reduce to 40% budget in winter. Focus on patio/hardscaping keywords in off-season. |
| Cleaning | Pre-holiday, January | Mid-summer | Increase 30% before holidays and in January. Shift to deep-cleaning keywords in summer. |

Use Google Trends to track search volume patterns for your core keywords in your specific region. Search "boiler repair" in Berlin versus Barcelona and you will see dramatically different seasonal curves. Calibrate your budget shifts 2-3 weeks before peak season starts — not when it is already underway.

## Lead Quality and Qualification Tactics

Getting 100 leads per month means nothing if 60 of them are price shoppers, DIYers looking for free advice, or people outside your service area. Lead quality separates profitable campaigns from budget drains.

**Write ad copy that filters before they call.** Instead of "Best Plumber in Town — Call Now," use "Certified Gas Boiler Repair — Free Quote, Same-Day Service." The specificity attracts homeowners with actual boiler problems and repels people looking for a quick DIY tip. Include your starting price point if regulations allow — "Drain Unblocking from €95" pre-qualifies buyers at your price level.

On your landing page or in your phone greeting, ask three qualifying questions before committing to a visit:

1. **What is the postcode?** — Confirm they are in your service area.
2. **What type of property and what is the issue?** — Determine if it matches your expertise.
3. **When do you need the work done?** — Urgency indicates booking likelihood and helps you prioritise.

Track lead quality as a metric, not just lead volume. Rate each inquiry on a 1-5 scale after the call: 5 = booked job, 4 = strong quote opportunity, 3 = possible but price-sensitive, 2 = wrong service area or type, 1 = tyre-kicker or free advice seeker. If your campaign generates 80 leads but 40 are rated 1-2, you are wasting half your budget. Use this data to refine keywords, adjust ad copy, and add negative keywords.

## Measuring What Actually Matters

Clicks and impressions are vanity metrics for trades businesses. The only numbers that matter are cost per lead, lead-to-quote rate, quote-to-job rate, and return on ad spend (ROAS).

Set up conversion tracking properly before you spend a single euro. Track three conversion actions at minimum: phone calls from ads, phone calls from your website, and contact form submissions. Use Google forwarding numbers to track calls from ads — they are free and tie every call to a specific keyword and ad.

Import offline conversions if possible. When a lead becomes a paying customer, push that data back into Google Ads. Google's Smart Bidding algorithms learn from actual revenue, not just form fills. A campaign optimising for closed jobs rather than raw leads typically sees 20-35% improvement in cost per acquisition within 60 days.

**Minimum viable reporting — check weekly:**

1. Cost per lead by campaign
2. Search terms report — add negative keywords for irrelevant queries
3. Location report — exclude underperforming postcodes
4. Call duration and call-to-booking rate
5. ROAS calculation: Revenue from ad-driven jobs ÷ Ad spend

Do not make changes more than once per week. Google's algorithm needs 5-7 days of data to learn from any adjustment. Frequent tweaking prevents the system from optimising.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between Google Ads and Local Services Ads for home services?

Google Ads (Search) operates on a pay-per-click model where you choose keywords, write ads, and pay when someone clicks. Local Services Ads use a pay-per-lead model — you pay only when a customer calls or messages through the ad. LSAs appear above regular ads and display the Google Guaranteed badge after verification. Search Ads offer more control over targeting and messaging. LSAs require less management but demand a verified business profile and minimum reviews. We recommend running both once your LSA profile is approved.

### How much should a trades business spend on Google Ads per month?

A single-location trades business in a mid-size European city should budget €1,500-€2,500 per month for meaningful lead volume. Below €1,000, you will not generate enough data for Google's algorithm to optimise effectively. Above €3,000, consider expanding to additional campaign types like Performance Max. Start with €2,000, prove profitability over 60-90 days, then scale based on your capacity to handle more jobs.

### Why am I getting clicks but no phone calls from my Google Ads?

This usually means one of four things: your ads run outside business hours and go to voicemail; your ad copy does not include a phone number or call extension; your landing page buries the contact information; or your targeting is too broad. Fix this by adding call extensions, scheduling ads during answering hours only, adding click-to-call buttons to your mobile landing page, and tightening your keyword match types to high-intent phrases only.

### How long does it take to see results from Google Ads for home services?

Search Ads can generate leads within 48 hours of launch. However, optimal performance typically takes 3-6 weeks as Google's algorithm learns which keywords and times of day produce conversions. Local Services Ads take 3-4 weeks just for verification. Do not judge performance before 30 days of consistent data. After 60 days, you should have clear cost-per-lead figures and be able to calculate your return on ad spend.

### Should I hire an agency or manage Google Ads myself?

If you have 5-10 hours per week to learn the platform, monitor search terms, adjust bids, and track conversions, self-management is possible. Most trades business owners do not have that time.
