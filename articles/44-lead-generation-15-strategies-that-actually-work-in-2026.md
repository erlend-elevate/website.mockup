# Lead Generation: 15 Strategies That Actually Work in 2026

Not every lead generation strategy works for every service business. A tactic that generates cheap leads for a home services company may be useless for a B2B consultancy. This guide presents 15 **lead generation strategies** rated by speed, cost, effort, and lead quality — so you can choose what fits your business.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **SEO and Google Ads are the foundation** — they work for almost every service business.
> - **Email marketing delivers the highest ROI** — 36:1 according to DMA.
> - **Partnerships and referrals** generate the highest-quality leads at the lowest cost.
> - **Speed and quality are inversely correlated** — fast leads are often lower quality.
> - **No single channel is enough** — the best results come from 3–5 channels working together.

## Fast Strategies (Results in 1–4 Weeks)

### 1. Google Ads
Target high-intent keywords like "hire [service] [city]." Immediate visibility, pay-per-click, fully trackable.

### 2. Meta Ads
Best for local and visual services. Target by demographics, interests, and behaviours.

### 14. Retargeting
Show ads to people who visited your website but did not convert. Highest ROI of any paid channel.

### 15. Cold Calling
Labour-intensive but effective for B2B. Requires trained callers and targeted lists.

## Medium-Term Strategies (Results in 1–3 Months)

### 4. LinkedIn Outreach
Personalised connection requests and messages to ideal prospects. Best for B2B consulting and professional services.

### 5. Email Marketing
Nurture sequences for inbound leads. Highest ROI channel — 36:1.

### 7. Webinars
Position expertise, generate qualified leads. Best for complex B2B services.

### 11. Directories
Industry-specific and local directories. Set-and-forget with ongoing lead flow.

### 13. Video Marketing
YouTube and LinkedIn video content. Builds trust and generates inbound enquiries.

## Slow but Compounding Strategies (Results in 3–12 Months)

### 1. SEO
Compounds over time. Once ranking, generates leads at near-zero marginal cost.

### 6. Content Marketing
Blog posts, guides, and resources that attract organic traffic and establish authority.

### 8. Referrals
Systematic referral programmes. Highest-quality leads once established.

### 9. Partnerships
Co-marketing with complementary businesses. Shared audiences, shared costs.

### 10. Local Events
Networking, speaking, and sponsoring. Builds relationships that convert over time.

### 12. Guest Posting
Contribute to industry publications. Builds authority and generates referral traffic.

## FAQ

### What is the fastest way to generate leads?
Google Ads for high-intent keywords. You can generate leads within 1–2 weeks of campaign launch.

### What is the cheapest lead generation strategy?
Referrals and partnerships. Both leverage existing relationships and require minimal direct cost.

### Which strategy has the best lead quality?
Referrals and LinkedIn outreach generate the highest-quality leads because they come with built-in trust.

### How many lead generation channels should I use?
3–5 channels is optimal for most service businesses. Fewer = vulnerability. More = diluted focus.

### Should I do lead generation in-house or outsource?
Start in-house to learn what works. Outsource channels that require specialist expertise (Google Ads, SEO) or scale beyond your capacity.
