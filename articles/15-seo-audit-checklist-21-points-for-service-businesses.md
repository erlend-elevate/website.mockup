# SEO Audit Checklist: 21 Points for Service Businesses

An SEO audit is the single most valuable diagnostic tool for any service business investing in organic growth. Without a systematic audit, you are optimising blindly — fixing symptoms while the underlying disease spreads. This **seo audit checklist** covers 21 essential points across four categories: Technical, On-Page, Off-Page, and Local SEO.

Each point includes what to check, how to check it, what "good" looks like, and estimated fix time. Use this as a monthly, quarterly, or pre-launch diagnostic.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **Technical SEO (7 points)** ensures search engines can crawl, render, and index your site efficiently.
> - **On-Page SEO (7 points)** optimises individual pages for target keywords and user experience.
> - **Off-Page SEO (4 points)** evaluates your backlink profile and domain authority.
> - **Local SEO (3 points)** checks your Google Business Profile and local citation consistency.

## Technical SEO (7 Points)

### 1. Crawlability & robots.txt
**What to check:** Is your robots.txt blocking important pages or allowing low-value ones? Are meta robots tags set correctly?
**How to check:** Google Search Console → Coverage report; Screaming Frog crawl
**Good:** No unintended blocks; CSS/JS not blocked; money pages crawlable
**Fix:** Update robots.txt; remove noindex from important pages

### 2. HTTPS & Security
**What to check:** Is your entire site on HTTPS? Any mixed content warnings?
**How to check:** SSL Labs test; Screaming Frog protocol report
**Good:** A+ SSL rating; no mixed content; HSTS enabled
**Fix:** Force HTTPS redirects; update internal links; fix mixed content

### 3. Core Web Vitals
**What to check:** LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1
**How to check:** PageSpeed Insights; Search Console → Core Web Vitals report
**Good:** All metrics in "Good" range for 75%+ of pages
**Fix:** Compress images, enable CDN, defer JS, optimise fonts

### 4. Mobile-Friendliness
**What to check:** Does every page render correctly on mobile? Tap targets adequate?
**How to check:** Google Mobile-Friendly Test; Search Console → Mobile Usability
**Good:** No mobile errors; viewport set; tap targets ≥48px
**Fix:** Responsive design audit; fix viewport issues; increase tap targets

### 5. XML Sitemap
**What to check:** Is your sitemap submitted to Google? Only indexable URLs included?
**How to check:** Search Console → Sitemaps; Screaming Frog sitemap analysis
**Good:** Clean sitemap; <50,000 URLs; no 404s or redirects
**Fix:** Remove parameterised URLs; resubmit after changes

### 6. Canonical Tags
**What to check:** Does every page have a self-referencing canonical? Are parameters handled?
**How to check:** Screaming Frog → Canonical column
**Good:** 100% canonical coverage; no conflicting signals
**Fix:** Add missing canonicals; implement parameter canonicalisation

### 7. Structured Data / Schema
**What to check:** Is LocalBusiness, Organization, and relevant schema implemented?
**How to check:** Google's Rich Results Test; Schema.org validator
**Good:** No errors; key pages have appropriate markup
**Fix:** Add JSON-LD schema; validate and test

## On-Page SEO (7 Points)

### 8. Title Tags
**What to check:** Unique, keyword-optimised titles under 60 characters on every page?
**How to check:** Screaming Frog → Title tag report
**Good:** Primary keyword in first 3 words; brand at end; no duplicates
**Fix:** Rewrite duplicate/short/long titles; add keywords

### 9. Meta Descriptions
**What to check:** Compelling descriptions under 155 characters with CTA?
**How to check:** Screaming Frog → Meta Description report
**Good:** Unique; includes keyword; action-oriented; under 155 chars
**Fix:** Rewrite missing/duplicate descriptions; add CTAs

### 10. Header Tags (H1–H3)
**What to check:** One H1 per page; logical H2/H3 hierarchy; keywords included?
**How to check:** Screaming Frog → H1/H2 report; manual page review
**Good:** Single H1; descriptive headings; keyword variations
**Fix:** Consolidate multiple H1s; rewrite vague headings

### 11. Keyword Cannibalisation
**What to check:** Are multiple pages targeting the same primary keyword?
**How to check:** Ahrefs/SEMrush cannibalisation report; manual audit
**Good:** One primary page per keyword; supporting content clearly differentiated
**Fix:** Merge competing pages; canonicalise; differentiate targeting

### 12. Content Quality & Depth
**What to check:** Does content match search intent? Sufficient depth? Original?
**How to check:** Manual review against top 3 ranking pages
**Good:** Comprehensive; original insights; matches intent; well-structured
**Fix:** Expand thin content; add original research; improve formatting

### 13. Internal Linking Structure
**What to check:** Are important pages linked from high-authority pages? Anchor text relevant?
**How to check:** Screaming Frog → Inlinks report
**Good:** 3–5 internal links per page; descriptive anchor text; logical flow
**Fix:** Add contextual links; improve anchor text; orphan page remediation

### 14. Image Optimisation
**What to check:** All images compressed? Alt text descriptive? WebP format?
**How to check:** Screaming Frog → Images report; PageSpeed Insights
**Good:** <100KB per image; descriptive alt text; WebP where supported
**Fix:** Compress images; add alt text; convert to WebP

## Off-Page SEO (4 Points)

### 15. Backlink Profile Quality
**What to check:** Do you have links from authoritative, relevant domains?
**How to check:** Ahrefs/SEMrush backlink analysis
**Good:** Diverse referring domains; relevant industry links; authority score growing
**Fix:** Digital PR; guest posting; partnership outreach

### 16. Referring Domain Diversity
**What to check:** How many unique domains link to you? Too many from one source?
**How to check:** Ahrefs → Referring Domains report
**Good:** Links from 50+ unique domains; no single domain >10% of total
**Fix:** Diversify link sources; avoid site-wide links

### 17. Toxic Link Audit
**What to check:** Any spammy, paid, or manipulative links pointing to your site?
**How to check:** Ahrefs/SEMrush toxicity score; manual review
**Good:** Toxic score <10%; no manual actions in Search Console
**Fix:** Disavow toxic links; document removal attempts

### 18. Brand Mention Monitoring
**What to check:** Are people mentioning your brand without linking?
**How to check:** Google Alerts; Ahrefs → Mentions
**Good:** Regular unlinked mentions identified and converted
**Fix:** Outreach to convert mentions to links

## Local SEO (3 Points)

### 19. Google Business Profile
**What to check:** Verified? Complete? Active with posts and reviews?
**How to check:** Google Business Profile dashboard
**Good:** 100% complete; weekly posts; all reviews responded
**Fix:** Complete missing fields; start posting; respond to reviews

### 20. NAP Consistency
**What to check:** Name, Address, Phone identical across all listings?
**How to check:** Moz Local; BrightLocal; manual directory check
**Good:** 100% consistent NAP across top 20+ directories
**Fix:** Update inconsistent listings; standardise formatting

### 21. Local Citation Presence
**What to check:** Are you listed in relevant local and industry directories?
**How to check:** BrightLocal citation tracker; manual search
**Good:** 15–30 quality citations; relevant to industry and geography
**Fix:** Submit to missing directories; remove duplicate listings

## FAQ

### How often should I run an SEO audit?
Quarterly for established sites, monthly for active campaigns, and immediately after any major site changes, redesigns, or algorithm updates.

### What tools do I need for an SEO audit?
Essential: Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, Screaming Frog (free up to 500 URLs). Recommended: Ahrefs or SEMrush, PageSpeed Insights, Moz Local.

### How long does a full SEO audit take?
A thorough audit of a 50-page service business website takes 8–16 hours. A 200+ page site can take 20–40 hours. Implementation typically takes 2–4 weeks.

### What is the most common issue found in SEO audits?
Thin or duplicate content combined with poor internal linking. Most service business websites have adequate technical foundations but fail on content depth and strategic keyword targeting.

### Should I hire an agency for an SEO audit?
If you have in-house SEO expertise, you can conduct the audit yourself. If not, an agency audit (typically €1,500–€5,000) will identify issues faster and provide a prioritised action plan.
