# How Much Should a Professional Website Cost in 2026?

You need a new website. Maybe your current one looks dated, converts poorly, or was built by your cousin in 2019. Whatever the reason, the first question is always the same: what will it cost?

The honest answer: anywhere from €0 (if you build it yourself on a free plan) to €100,000+ (if you hire a premium agency for a complex project). For most European service businesses, the realistic range is €2,000–€25,000 depending on scope, complexity, and who builds it.

This guide breaks down **professional website cost** by approach, scope, and geography. We include hidden costs most estimates forget, an ROI framework, and specific recommendations for service businesses.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **DIY builders cost €10–€50/month** but require significant time and deliver limited functionality.
> - **Freelancers charge €1,500–€8,000** for custom designs. Quality varies enormously.
> - **Agencies charge €5,000–€50,000+** depending on scope, strategy, and ongoing support.
> - **Hidden costs** (hosting, maintenance, plugins, content) add €500–€3,000/year.
> - **A website should generate 3–10x its cost in annual revenue** to be considered a good investment.

## What Affects Website Cost

**Custom design vs. template:** Custom designs cost 3–5x more but deliver unique branding and optimised conversion flows. Templates are faster and cheaper but less distinctive.

**CMS platform:** WordPress (most flexible, higher maintenance), Webflow (designer-friendly, moderate cost), Framer (modern, developer-friendly), custom build (most expensive, most control).

**Number of pages:** A 5-page brochure site costs significantly less than a 50-page site with multiple service areas, case studies, and blog content.

**Integrations:** CRM connections, booking systems, payment gateways, marketing automation — each adds development time and cost.

**E-commerce:** Online stores require product pages, cart functionality, payment processing, and inventory management — adding €3,000–€15,000.

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest way to get a professional website?
DIY builders (Wix, Squarespace) start at €10–€20/month. However, the limitations in SEO, customisation, and scalability often cost more in lost opportunities than the upfront savings.

### How much does a 5-page website cost?
Freelancer: €1,500–€4,000. Agency: €4,000–€10,000. The range depends on design complexity, copywriting needs, and whether SEO is included.

### Should I pay monthly or upfront for a website?
Most agencies require 50% upfront, 25% at design approval, and 25% at launch. Monthly retainers for ongoing maintenance are standard. Avoid agencies demanding 100% upfront.

### How long does it take to build a professional website?
DIY: 1–4 weeks. Freelancer: 2–8 weeks. Agency: 6–16 weeks. Complex projects with custom functionality can take 6+ months.

### What is the most important factor in website cost?
Scope clarity. The more precisely you define what you need — pages, features, integrations, content — the more accurate your quote. Vague briefs lead to cost overruns and timeline delays.
