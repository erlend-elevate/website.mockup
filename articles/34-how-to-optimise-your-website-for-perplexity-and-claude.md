# How to Optimise Your Website for Perplexity and Claude

Perplexity and Claude are two of the most influential AI platforms for research and decision-making. Perplexity processes over 100 million queries monthly, while Claude powers enterprise research workflows. If your business is not optimised for these platforms, you are invisible to a growing segment of AI-native users.

This guide covers technical, content, and brand strategies to **optimise for perplexity** and Claude, with practical implementation steps.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **Perplexity prioritises recent, well-sourced content** from authoritative domains.
> - **Claude values depth, clarity, and structured reasoning** in the content it cites.
> - **Both platforms rely heavily on structured data** to understand page context.
> - **Freshness matters** — regularly updated content is cited more frequently.
> - **Brand authority signals** (mentions, reviews, citations) influence AI recommendations.

## How Perplexity and Claude Discover Content

Both platforms use a combination of web crawling, API integrations, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to find and cite content. They prioritise:

1. **Authoritative domains** with strong backlink profiles
2. **Structured content** with clear headings, lists, and tables
3. **Recent publications** (especially for time-sensitive topics)
4. **Original data and research** not available elsewhere
5. **Consistent business information** across multiple sources

## Technical Optimisation

### 1. Structured Data Implementation

| Schema Type | Purpose | Implementation |
|-------------|---------|---------------|
| Organization | Brand identity | JSON-LD in homepage header |
| LocalBusiness | Geographic relevance | Include on contact/location pages |
| FAQPage | Question-answer pairs | Add to service and blog pages |
| HowTo | Process content | Step-by-step guides |
| Article | Blog posts | Author, date, publisher markup |
| Review | Social proof | Aggregate rating markup |

### 2. Content Freshness Signals

- Publish date visible on all content
- "Last updated" timestamp for evergreen articles
- Regular content audits and updates
- Fresh content published weekly

### 3. Clear Information Architecture

- Logical URL structure
- Descriptive headings (H1, H2, H3)
- Breadcrumb navigation
- XML sitemap submitted to search engines

## Content Optimisation

### 1. Answer-First Paragraphs

Every piece of content should answer the core question in the first 2–3 sentences.

**Example:**
> *"Performance Max is Google's AI-driven campaign type that automates ad placement across Search, Display, YouTube, and Discover. It works best for businesses with 30+ monthly conversions and high-quality creative assets."*

### 2. Structured Lists and Tables

AI models parse structured data more easily than prose. Use:
- Bulleted lists for features/benefits
- Numbered lists for processes
- Comparison tables for alternatives
- Data tables for statistics

### 3. Definition-Rich Content

Define key terms before going deeper. AI models lift definitions verbatim.

**Format:** "[Term] is [simple definition]. [Expansion with detail]."

### 4. Original Research and Data

Publish proprietary research, surveys, and benchmarks. Original data is uncitable from other sources, making your content uniquely valuable to AI models.

## FAQ

### How do I know if Perplexity is citing my content?
Search Perplexity for queries related to your expertise. Check if your brand or content appears in responses and citations.

### Does optimising for Perplexity help with Google SEO?
Yes. Most Perplexity optimisation tactics (structured data, clear content, authoritative backlinks) also improve traditional SEO.

### How often should I update content for AI search?
Update evergreen content quarterly. Publish new content weekly. AI models prioritise fresh, current information.

### What types of content do AI models cite most?
FAQ pages, definition guides, original research, comparison articles, and step-by-step tutorials with clear structure.

### Is there a way to directly submit my site to Perplexity or Claude?
No. These platforms crawl the web like search engines. Focus on creating excellent, structured content that naturally earns citations.
