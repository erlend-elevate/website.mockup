# 12 Most Common Google Ads Mistakes (and How to Avoid Them)

Most service businesses burn through their Google Ads budget before figuring out what went wrong. The platform is unforgiving — a single setup error can drain thousands of euros. We've audited 200+ campaigns at Elevate Marketing, and the same google ads mistakes surface repeatedly. Here are the 12 most common, with a fix for each.

> **TL;DR — The 12 Google Ads Mistakes at a Glance**
> - **No conversion tracking** — you're optimising for clicks, not results.
> - **Ignoring negative keywords** — your ads show for irrelevant searches.
> - **Bad match type strategy** — broad match without Smart Bidding wastes budget.
> - **Skipping ad extensions** — you're leaving free real estate on the table.
> - **Bidding on brand keywords wrong** — either overspending or handing traffic to competitors.
> - **Landing page mismatch** — high bounce rates kill your Quality Score and conversions.
> - **One ad group per campaign** — prevents relevant ad copy and hurts ad relevance.
> - **Ignoring Quality Score** — you're paying 20-50% more per click than you need to.
> - **No device bid adjustments** — mobile and desktop users convert at very different rates.
> - **Wrong geo-targeting** — showing ads outside your service area wastes spend.
> - **Set-it-and-forget-it management** — campaigns degrade without regular attention.
> - **No A/B testing** — you're guessing instead of knowing what works.

## Conversion Tracking Failures

### Mistake #1: Poor Conversion Tracking

This is the single costliest of all google ads mistakes. If you aren't measuring meaningful actions — form submissions, phone calls, purchases — you have no idea which keywords or campaigns drive revenue. Smart Bidding relies on conversion data; without it, every optimisation is a guess ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167132?hl=en)).

**The fix:** Install Google Tag Manager on every page. Track primary conversions separately from secondary ones. Set a value even for non-e-commerce actions — a lead form might be worth €50 if 1 in 10 closes for €500. Of all google ads errors, poor tracking is the easiest to fix and the most damaging to ignore. Verify with Google Tag Assistant before spending.

## Keyword Strategy Errors

These three google ads mistakes all relate to how you select and manage keywords — the foundation of every Search campaign.

### Mistake #2: Ignoring Negative Keywords

Without negative keywords, your ad for "corporate lawyer Frankfurt" shows up for "free legal aid Frankfurt." Every irrelevant click costs money and drags down your CTR. Negative keywords exclude search terms so you focus only on keywords that matter to customers ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453972?hl=en)).

**The fix:** Review Search Terms weekly. Build a master negative keyword list — "free," "cheap," "DIY," "jobs," "salary," "template" — and apply across all campaigns. Exclude region-specific terms outside your service area.

### Mistake #3: Bad Match Type Strategy

Using only broad match without Smart Bidding is handing Google a blank cheque. Using only exact match starves your campaign of volume. Average Google Ads conversion rates sit at roughly 6.96% across industries, but that drops sharply when broad match serves low-intent queries ([WordStream, 2024](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2024-google-ads-benchmarks)).

**The fix:** Start with phrase match for core keywords. Layer in broad match only when you have conversion data and Smart Bidding (Target CPA or Target ROAS) activated. Google recommends Smart Bidding with all match types — the algorithm learns faster with more data ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7478529?hl=en)). Never run broad match with manual CPC.

### Mistake #5: Bidding on Brand Keywords Wrong

Some agencies say never bid on your brand name. Others say always bid on it. Both are wrong. If competitors bid on your brand, you need defensive campaigns. If nobody does, you're wasting budget that should go to non-brand terms.

**The fix:** Search your brand on Google. If competitor ads appear above your organic result, launch a brand campaign — clicks should cost €0.10-€0.50, not €2-€5. If no competitors appear, pause brand bidding. Separate brand and non-brand campaigns to measure true acquisition.

## Campaign Structure and Targeting Errors

Poor structure and bad targeting are google ads mistakes that silently kill performance. Here's how to fix them.

### Mistake #7: One Ad Group Per Campaign (or Too Many Keywords per Group)

Stuffing 50 unrelated keywords into one ad group means your copy can't match every search intent. Google evaluates ad relevance by comparing ad text to keywords — mismatches hurt Quality Score ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167132?hl=en)).

**The fix:** Group keywords into tightly themed ad groups of 5 to 20 keywords, all sharing the same intent. "Commercial cleaning Munich," "office cleaning Munich," and "business cleaning Munich" belong together. "Home cleaning Munich" gets its own group. Tight groupings let you write hyper-relevant copy. These structural google ads mistakes are invisible in your dashboard but expensive in your invoice.

### Mistake #10: Wrong Geo-Targeting

We've seen a plumbing business targeting "all countries" when they only serve 30 kilometres around Vienna. We've seen "London" campaigns get clicks from London, Canada. Wrong geo-targeting burns budget on unconvertible clicks.

**The fix:** Set targeting to "People in or regularly in your targeted locations" — not "People interested in your targeted locations." The latter includes people abroad. Use radius targeting, exclude countries you don't serve, and check your User locations report monthly.

## Quality Score and Bidding Oversights

These google ads mistakes directly inflate your costs — every click becomes more expensive when you ignore them.

### Mistake #8: Ignoring Quality Score

Quality Score is Google's 1-10 rating of keyword relevance based on expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience. A low score means you pay more for every click. Improving a keyword's score from 3 to 7 can cut your CPC by 20-30%.

**The fix:** Add the Quality Score column to your keyword view. Sort by lowest score. For keywords below 5, diagnose the failing component — expected CTR, ad relevance, or landing page experience. Fix the weakest first: rewrite ads, tighten keyword groupings, or build dedicated pages.

### Mistake #9: No Device Bid Adjustments

Mobile users browse; desktop users buy. Same bids for both means overpaying for mobile or underbidding for desktop. Device experience factors into ad quality, so poor mobile performance hurts your whole account. These bidding google ads mistakes are quick to fix once you spot them.

**The fix:** Segment by device. If mobile cost per conversion doubles desktop, apply -30% to -50% on mobile. For B2B services, exclude tablets. Verify pages load in under 3 seconds on mobile.

## Ad Creative and Landing Page Gaps

Even experienced advertisers make these google ads mistakes — they focus on bidding and forget what users actually see.

### Mistake #4: Not Using Ad Extensions

Ad extensions — now called "assets" — give you extra text, links, and contact options at no extra cost. They increase visibility, improve CTR, and take up more space on search results. Skipping them is leaving half your billboard blank.

**The fix:** Add sitelinks (to service pages), callouts (key value props like "Free Consultation"), call extensions (for mobile), and structured snippets (service categories). Among all google ads mistakes, skipping extensions is the easiest fix — it takes 15 minutes and costs nothing. Ensure your privacy policy is GDPR-compliant for lead form extensions.

### Mistake #6: Landing Page Mismatch

Your ad promises "Corporate Tax Consulting — Free Initial Review" but the landing page is a generic homepage. The visitor bounces. Google registers poor landing page experience, your Quality Score drops, and CPC rises. The average conversion rate for business services in 2024 was 5.78% — only when the page matches the ad's promise ([WordStream, 2024](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2024-google-ads-benchmarks)).

**The fix:** Send every ad group to a dedicated landing page mirroring the ad's headline. If your ad says "family law solicitor in Berlin," the page headline should say exactly that. Over 60% of searches in Europe are mobile, so mobile optimisation is essential.

## Testing and Management Failures

The final two google ads mistakes are about ongoing management — where good campaigns go to die.

### Mistake #11: Set-It-and-Forget-It Mentality

Google Ads is not a billboard. Algorithms change, competitors shift bids, and search trends evolve. A campaign delivering €25 cost per lead in January can hit €80 by June unmaintained. We've seen new competitors double CPCs across verticals in a month.

**The fix:** Block 30 minutes weekly. Check Search Terms for negatives. Review impression share — drops mean competitors raised bids. Pause keywords with zero conversions after 100+ clicks. Adjust device, location, and time-of-day bids. These google ads mistakes compound over time; weekly maintenance prevents budget disasters.

### Mistake #12: No A/B Testing

Running one ad per ad group is guessing. Even a small change — "Free Consultation" versus "Free Strategy Call" — can lift CTR by 20%.

**The fix:** Run 2-3 responsive search ads per ad group. Test one variable: headline, offer, or call to action. Run to ~100 clicks per variant, then test a new challenger. After 5-10 tests, patterns emerge that fix the last of your google ads mistakes.

## FAQ

### What are the most expensive google ads mistakes for small budgets?

The costliest errors are poor conversion tracking and ignoring negative keywords. Without tracking, every optimisation is a guess. Without negatives, 10-30% of spend goes to irrelevant clicks. Fix these two first — they cost nothing and deliver immediate savings. For more [google ads best practices](/google-ads), start with tracking.

### How often should I check my Google Ads campaigns?

Spend 30 minutes per week on active campaigns. Review Search Terms, add negatives, adjust device and location bids, and pause keywords that spent significantly without converting. For new campaigns, check daily for the first two weeks. Monthly deep dives should cover landing pages and competitor analysis.

### What is a good Quality Score in Google Ads?

A Quality Score of 7+ is strong. Scores of 4-6 mean room for improvement. Scores of 1-3 are serious — your ads may stop showing, or CPC may be 50-100% higher. Fix "Below average" components first, as shown in keyword-level diagnostics.

### Should I use automated bidding or manual CPC?

Smart Bidding (Target CPA or Target ROAS) outperforms manual CPC once you have 30+ conversions per month. The algorithm optimises using signals you can't access. Below 30 conversions, use Enhanced CPC or Maximize Clicks with a CPC cap while building volume.

### How do I know if my landing page is the problem?

Check bounce rate (over 70% is a red flag), time on page (under 15 seconds suggests mismatch), and conversion rate (below 3-5%). If CTR is strong but conversions are weak, the landing page is the bottleneck — a google ads mistake we see in most audits. Match headlines to ad copy and simplify form fields.
