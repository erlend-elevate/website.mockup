# Marketing Mix Modelling for Small Businesses: Do You Need It?

**Marketing mix modelling** (MMM) is the statistical analysis of historical data to estimate the impact of various marketing activities on sales. Large enterprises have used MMM for decades to allocate billion-euro budgets. But for small service businesses, the question is different: is MMM worth the investment, or are simpler methods sufficient?

This guide explains what MMM is, when it makes sense for small businesses, and when alternatives deliver better ROI.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **MMM requires 2+ years of historical data and substantial marketing spend** — most small businesses lack both.
> - **Attribution modelling and incrementality testing** often provide sufficient insight at a fraction of the cost.
> - **MMM makes sense when you spend €50,000+/month across 5+ channels** with overlapping touchpoints.
> - **For most small service businesses, a simple correlation analysis in a spreadsheet** is sufficient.
> - **MMM tools start at €2,000–€10,000/month** — only viable for larger budgets.

## What Is Marketing Mix Modelling?

**Marketing mix modelling** uses statistical regression analysis to determine how various marketing inputs (ad spend, promotions, seasonality, economic factors) correlate with outputs (sales, revenue, leads). It answers: "If I increase Google Ads spend by 20%, what happens to revenue?"

**MMM typically measures:**
- Base sales (what you would sell with zero marketing)
- Incremental sales from each marketing channel
- Diminishing returns curves
- Carryover effects (how long marketing impact lasts)
- External factors (seasonality, competition, economy)

## MMM Decision Framework

| Factor | MMM Makes Sense | MMM Is Overkill |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|
| Monthly marketing spend | €50,000+ | <€10,000 |
| Channels active | 5+ | 1–3 |
| Historical data | 2+ years | <1 year |
| Internal analytics team | Yes | No |
| Budget for MMM tool/service | €2,000+/month | <€500/month |
| Attribution challenges | Complex overlap | Simple funnel |
| Business complexity | Multi-market, multi-product | Single market, single service |

## MMM vs. Alternatives

| Method | Cost | Data Required | Best For | Limitation |
|--------|------|--------------|----------|------------|
| Marketing Mix Modelling | €2,000–€10,000/month | 2+ years, 5+ channels | Large, complex businesses | Expensive, slow |
| Multi-touch Attribution | €200–€1,000/month | 6+ months, 3+ channels | Medium businesses | Cookie-dependent |
| Incrementality Testing | €500–€2,000/test | 4+ weeks per test | Any business with clear geo | Time-intensive |
| Simple Correlation | Free (spreadsheet) | 6+ months | Small businesses | Less precise |
| Cohort Analysis | Free (GA4/CRM) | 3+ months | Subscription/repeat services | Channel-agnostic |
| Surveys (Post-purchase) | €100–€500/month | Ongoing | All businesses | Self-reported bias |

## The Small Business Approach

For most service businesses spending under €10,000/month on marketing, this simple framework is sufficient:

1. **Track spend by channel** in a simple spreadsheet
2. **Track leads and revenue by source** in your CRM
3. **Calculate cost per lead and cost per client** by channel monthly
4. **Look for patterns** over 3–6 months
5. **Shift budget** from high-cost to low-cost channels
6. **Test incrementally** — change one variable at a time

## When to Consider MMM

Consider marketing mix modelling when:
- You spend €50,000+/month across multiple channels
- You operate in multiple markets with different dynamics
- Your attribution is genuinely complex (online + offline + brand)
- You have 2+ years of clean historical data
- You have an internal analytics team or budget for external support

## FAQ

### What is marketing mix modelling in simple terms?
A statistical method that analyses historical data to determine which marketing activities drive sales and by how much. It helps allocate budget across channels optimally.

### How much does marketing mix modelling cost?
€2,000–€10,000 per month for tools or consultants. Enterprise implementations can cost €50,000–€200,000 annually. For small businesses, simpler methods are more cost-effective.

### Do I need MMM if I use Google Analytics?
No. Google Analytics provides sufficient attribution data for most small businesses. Consider MMM only when GA4 cannot answer your allocation questions.

### What is the best alternative to MMM for small businesses?
Simple correlation analysis in a spreadsheet: track monthly spend by channel against leads and revenue. Over 6+ months, patterns emerge that guide allocation decisions.

### How long does MMM take to implement?
Traditional MMM: 3–6 months for the first model. Modern automated MMM tools: 2–4 weeks. Both require clean historical data.
