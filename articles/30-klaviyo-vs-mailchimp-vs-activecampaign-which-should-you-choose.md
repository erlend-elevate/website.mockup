# Klaviyo vs Mailchimp vs ActiveCampaign: Which Should You Choose?

Choosing an email marketing platform is a long-term decision. Migrating later costs time, money, and subscriber engagement. The right platform scales with your business, integrates with your tech stack, and keeps you GDPR-compliant. The wrong one becomes a constraint you fight against daily.

This guide compares three leading platforms — **klaviyo vs mailchimp vs activecampaign** — across ten criteria that matter for service businesses.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **Mailchimp is simplest** — best for small businesses sending basic newsletters.
> - **ActiveCampaign has the most powerful automation** — ideal for complex nurture sequences and CRM integration.
> - **Klaviyo excels at e-commerce** — overkill for most service businesses unless you sell products.
> - **ActiveCampaign is our top pick for service businesses** due to automation depth and lead scoring.
> - **GDPR compliance** requires more than the tool itself — you need proper consent processes.

## Mailchimp: The Simple Choice

**Best for:** Small service businesses (1–5 employees) sending monthly newsletters with basic automation.

**Pros:**
- Lowest learning curve — intuitive drag-and-drop builder
- Generous free tier (500 contacts, 1,000 sends/month)
- Built-in landing pages and basic website builder
- Good template library
- Well-known brand with extensive documentation

**Cons:**
- Automation is basic — no complex branching or conditional logic
- Segmentation limited compared to competitors
- Lead scoring not available on lower tiers
- Costs escalate quickly as you grow
- Customer support quality has declined since Intuit acquisition

**Mailchimp pricing (EUR, 2026):**
- Free: 500 contacts, 1,000 sends/month
- Essentials: €13–€27/month (growing list)
- Standard: €20–€69/month (automation + segmentation)
- Premium: €350+/month (advanced segmentation + support)

**When to choose:** You send 1–2 emails per month, have simple automation needs, and want the easiest platform to learn.

## ActiveCampaign: The Automation Powerhouse

**Best for:** Service businesses with structured sales processes, nurture sequences, and CRM integration needs.

**Pros:**
- Most powerful automation builder of the three — conditional logic, branching, goal-based sequences
- Built-in CRM with pipeline management
- Advanced lead scoring (engagement + behavioural)
- Excellent segmentation — segment by behaviour, engagement, custom fields
- Site tracking connects email behaviour to website activity
- Strong reporting and attribution
- EU data residency available
- 900+ integrations

**Cons:**
- Steeper learning curve than Mailchimp
- Higher starting price
- Landing page builder is functional but not beautiful
- Can feel overwhelming for simple needs

**ActiveCampaign pricing (EUR, 2026):**
- Lite: €29–€79/month (basic automation)
- Plus: €49–€179/month (CRM + lead scoring)
- Professional: €149–€399/month (predictive sending + attribution)
- Enterprise: Custom (dedicated support + customisation)

**When to choose:** You have a defined sales process, want sophisticated nurture sequences, need CRM integration, or plan to scale beyond 5,000 subscribers.

## Klaviyo: The E-Commerce Specialist

**Best for:** Service businesses that also sell products, courses, or digital goods. Overkill for pure service businesses.

**Pros:**
- Exceptional e-commerce integration (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento)
- Advanced segmentation based on purchase behaviour
- Excellent reporting and predictive analytics
- Generous free tier (250 contacts, 500 emails/month)
- Strong A/B testing capabilities
- Good deliverability rates

**Cons:**
- Automation is powerful but e-commerce-centric
- CRM capabilities limited
- Overkill if you don't sell products
- Learning curve for non-e-commerce use cases
- Support can be slow on lower tiers

**Klaviyo pricing (EUR, 2026):**
- Free: 250 contacts, 500 emails/month
- Email: €20–€1,700+/month (based on list size)
- Email + SMS: Additional cost per SMS

**When to choose:** You sell products alongside services, run an e-commerce operation, or need deep purchase behaviour segmentation.

## FAQ

### Which is cheapest for a small list?
Mailchimp has the lowest paid tier (€13/month). Klaviyo has the best free tier (250 contacts). For under 500 subscribers on a budget, start with Mailchimp Free or Klaviyo Free.

### Can I switch platforms later?
Yes, but migration requires exporting contacts, rebuilding automations, and re-creating templates. Plan for 1–2 weeks of transition work. Export subscriber data regularly as backup.

### Which has the best automation?
ActiveCampaign, by a significant margin. Its visual automation builder supports complex branching, conditional logic, goal tracking, and lead scoring that Mailchimp and Klaviyo cannot match.

### Is Klaviyo good for service businesses?
Only if you also sell products. For pure service businesses, ActiveCampaign's CRM integration and service-focused automation make it a better choice.

### Do all three handle GDPR?
Yes, but ActiveCampaign has the most robust EU data handling features. For strict GDPR compliance, verify EU data residency and sign a data processing agreement with your chosen platform.
