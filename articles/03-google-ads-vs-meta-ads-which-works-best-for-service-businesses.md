# Google Ads vs Meta Ads: Which Works Best for Service Businesses?

You are burning €2,000 a month on ads. The leads come in, but half of them never pick up the phone. Your cost per qualified lead is creeping upward, and you are starting to wonder whether you chose the wrong platform entirely. This is the exact dilemma we see every week at Elevate Marketing. Service businesses — law firms, consultancies, clinics, home services, B2B agencies — running campaigns without a clear answer to one question: are we advertising where our best clients actually pay attention? This article gives you a direct, data-backed **google ads vs meta ads** comparison for service businesses in the EU market. You will learn how each platform works, what it costs, which delivers better leads, and how to decide where your budget belongs in this **google ads vs meta ads** decision.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **Google Ads captures existing demand.** Your prospects are already searching — you just need to show up at the right moment.
> - **Meta Ads creates new demand.** You reach people who match your ideal client profile but are not actively searching yet.
> - **Google Ads typically costs more per click but converts better** for high-intent service queries.
> - **Meta Ads offers lower entry costs and stronger visual storytelling**, ideal for trust-building.
> - **Most service businesses perform best using both platforms together**, not choosing one over the other.

## How Google Ads and Meta Ads Work Differently

Google Ads is a demand-capture platform. Someone types "family lawyer near me" or "emergency plumber Berlin" into Google. They already have a problem, they know what solution looks like, and they are actively looking for a provider. Your ad appears at the exact moment of intent. This is why [Google Ads](/google-ads) consistently delivers the highest conversion rates of any paid channel for service businesses.

According to [WordStream's 2024 industry benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2024-google-ads-benchmarks), business services on Google Search see an average conversion rate of 5.78%. Home improvement categories hit 8.62%, and legal services convert at 5.64%. When someone searches for a service, they are already in buying mode.

Meta Ads is a demand-creation platform. Your ads appear in Facebook and Instagram feeds, Stories, Reels, and Messenger. You are placing your message in front of people who match your target audience but were not necessarily looking for you. Your creative does the heavy lifting — a video testimonial, a before-and-after image, or a carousel ad interrupts the scroll and sparks interest.

Meta's targeting is behaviour-based. You define audiences by demographics, interests, job titles, and behaviours, then layer on lookalike audiences — people who resemble your best existing clients. A consultancy targeting CFOs at mid-market manufacturers can reach exactly that group. A home services company can target homeowners in specific postcodes who have shown interest in renovation content.

The core distinction in any **google ads vs meta ads** comparison is this: Google responds to intent, Meta creates it. That single difference shapes every metric downstream.

## Cost Reality: What EU Service Businesses Actually Spend

We manage campaigns across legal, consulting, healthcare, home services, and B2B sectors in EU markets. Here is what typical costs look like when you compare **google ads vs meta ads** spending side by side.

**Google Ads costs for EU service businesses:**
- Legal services: €6-€18 per click depending on city and specialisation
- Healthcare / dental: €3-€8 per click
- B2B consulting: €4-€12 per click
- Home services: €2-€6 per click
- Local professional services: €2-€5 per click

**Meta Ads costs for the same sectors:**
- Legal services: €0.80-€2.50 per click
- Healthcare / dental: €0.50-€1.50 per click
- B2B consulting: €0.80-€2.00 per click
- Home services: €0.40-€1.20 per click

Cost per lead tells a different story than cost per click. On Google, a law firm in a major EU city might pay €80-€200 per lead. On Meta, the same firm might pay €30-€70 per lead. But lead quality differs. Google leads often close at 15-25% because the prospect initiated the search. Meta leads might close at 5-12% because they responded to an ad while scrolling.

This is why we track cost per qualified lead and cost per acquired client, not just form submissions. A €150 Google lead that closes 1 in 4 times beats a €40 Meta lead that closes 1 in 20. When evaluating **google ads vs meta ads** purely on cost, always account for your actual close rate from each source.

## When to Choose Google Ads vs Meta Ads

Understanding when each platform wins is the key to making the **google ads vs meta ads** decision for your business. Here is how we think about it.

### Google Ads wins when:

**Your prospects are already searching.** If there is measurable search volume for your service — "employment lawyer Amsterdam," "tax consultant Munich," "emergency locksmith Paris" — Google Ads puts you in front of active buyers. No other platform matches this intent.

**Urgency drives the purchase decision.** Home services, legal emergencies, medical appointments, and breakdown repairs follow this pattern. The person with a burst pipe or a deadline for legal appeal is not browsing Instagram. They are on Google, now.

**Lifetime value justifies higher acquisition costs.** A B2B consultancy with €15,000 average project value can absorb a €200 cost per lead. A corporate law firm billing €300 hourly can afford €8 clicks. When margins are strong, paying more for high-intent traffic makes mathematical sense.

In our experience, local service businesses see the fastest ROI on Google Ads. A dental clinic we work with achieved a 6.2x ROAS on Search campaigns within 60 days by targeting high-intent keywords like "dental implant consultation" with dedicated landing pages — a clear example of why **google ads vs meta ads** is not just about cost per click. The cost per appointment booked was €35, and the average treatment value exceeded €2,000.

### Meta Ads wins when:

**You need to build awareness before demand exists.** If you offer a novel service, enter a new market, or target a niche that does not yet search for your specific solution, Meta lets you create demand. A sustainability consultancy helping manufacturers meet EU compliance targets cannot rely on search volume for a service many prospects do not know exists yet.

**Visual proof and trust-building drive your sale.** Interior designers, architects, cosmetic clinics, fitness coaches, and premium home services all benefit from before-and-after imagery, client testimonials, and video content. Meta's formats are built for this. A carousel showing three kitchen transformations will outperform a text ad every time.

**Your target audience is definable but not actively searching.** B2B services targeting specific job roles, demographic-specific services, and interest-based niches all perform well on Meta. You reach the right person based on who they are, not what they typed. [Learn more about our Meta Ads approach here](/meta-ads).

Meta also excels at retargeting. A prospect visits your website from Google, does not convert, and sees your follow-up ad on Instagram three days later. This cross-platform synergy is where much of Meta's value lives. When you weigh **google ads vs meta ads** for audience-building, Meta's visual formats and retargeting give it a clear edge in the discovery phase.

## The Case for Running Both Platforms Together

Here is the answer most service businesses need: do not choose one. Run both, with clear roles for each.

A combined Google and Meta strategy works like a relay race. Google Ads captures the people actively searching right now. Meta Ads builds awareness with people who will search later, and retargets those who visited your site but did not convert. Together, they cover the full funnel.

Research from PPC analysis platforms shows that running both platforms together can reduce overall cost per acquisition by 15-25% compared to running either in isolation. A lead might discover you on Meta, verify you on Google, and convert after seeing a retargeting ad. You benefit from reaching prospects at multiple touchpoints.

**A practical budget split for a service business spending €3,000-€5,000 per month:**

| Funnel Stage | Platform | Role | Suggested Budget Split |
|-------------|----------|------|----------------------|
| High-intent capture | Google Ads Search | Convert active searchers | 40-50% |
| Awareness / discovery | Meta Ads | Build audience, create demand | 25-35% |
| Retargeting | Meta + Google Display | Re-engage non-converters | 15-25% |

This allocation captures existing demand while systematically building new demand. As your brand recognition grows, more people will search for you by name — and your Google Ads costs will drop as branded search volume increases. This is why the best **google ads vs meta ads** strategy often uses both platforms in combination rather than treating them as competitors.

The key requirement for running both platforms? Proper tracking. You need the Meta Pixel, Google Tag, and ideally server-side tracking to attribute leads correctly. Without this, you are flying blind on which platform actually drove the conversion. If you are struggling to make this **google ads vs meta ads** choice, start with your tracking setup — accurate data makes the decision obvious.

## 3 Mistakes That Kill Campaigns on Both Platforms

**Optimising for clicks instead of qualified leads.** A high click-through rate means nothing if the clicks come from people who cannot afford your service or are outside your area. We see businesses bragging about "low CPC" while their cost per qualified lead destroys their margin. Track lead quality, not just volume. This mistake undermines **google ads vs meta ads** performance on either platform.

**Sending traffic to generic homepages.** Your homepage has too many exits. A Google Ads campaign for "family law consultation" should land on a page about family law consultations, with a clear form or booking button and trust signals. Dedicated landing pages consistently improve conversion rates by 40-100% versus homepage sends.

**Giving up during the learning phase.** Both platforms need data to optimise. Google Ads campaigns typically need 2-4 weeks and 30+ conversions to stabilise. Meta's algorithm needs 50 optimisation events per week to exit the learning phase. Changing your strategy every three days prevents either platform from working. Set your tracking, launch, gather data, then optimise. Patience is a targeting parameter.

## Tracking and Attribution: The Make-or-Break Factor

You cannot compare **google ads vs meta ads** fairly without accurate tracking. Yet most service businesses we audit have significant gaps here.

Google Ads and Meta use different attribution models by default. Google's data-driven attribution distributes credit across touchpoints. Meta defaults to 7-day click, 1-day view. Someone who clicks a Meta ad on Monday and converts via a Google search on Wednesday might be counted by both platforms. Both claim the conversion.

For EU businesses, GDPR compliance adds complexity. You need explicit consent for tracking cookies and pixels. Your cookie banner must properly integrate with your ad pixels. Server-side tracking (Meta's Conversions API and Google's enhanced conversions) reduces reliance on browser cookies and improves data accuracy by 20-40% according to Meta's own documentation. [Average CPCs in Western Europe](https://www.hunchads.com/blog/facebook-ads-cost) vary significantly by country, so accurate attribution matters even more when costs differ across markets.

Our recommendation: set up a unified dashboard in Google Analytics 4 that pulls cost and conversion data from both platforms. Use UTM parameters on every ad. Define one source of truth for "a lead" — typically your CRM — and compare platform-reported numbers against actual signed clients. Platform ROAS is an indicator. CRM-reported client acquisition cost is the truth. Getting your **google ads vs meta ads** comparison right depends on having clean, unified data. This tracking foundation is what separates successful service business ppc strategy from wasted ad spend.

## FAQ

### Which is cheaper per lead, Google Ads or Meta Ads?
Meta Ads usually delivers a lower cost per raw lead — often 40-60% cheaper than Google Ads for service businesses. However, Google leads typically close at 2-3x the rate because they come from active searchers. Your actual cost per acquired client depends on your close rate from each source. Track qualified leads and signed clients, not just form submissions, to compare the true cost efficiency of **google ads vs meta ads** for your specific business.

### How long does it take to see results from each platform?
Google Ads can generate qualified leads within 1-2 weeks if your keywords, landing pages, and tracking are set up correctly. Meta Ads typically needs 2-4 weeks to exit the learning phase and deliver stable results. For both platforms, plan 60-90 days to gather enough data for meaningful optimisation. The first month is almost always a data-gathering phase.

### Should a new service business start with Google Ads or Meta Ads?
Start with Google Ads if people are already searching for your specific service. Start with Meta Ads if you are introducing something new, targeting a niche audience, or building awareness in a market where search volume is low. If your budget is under €2,000 per month, focus on one platform and do it well rather than splitting resources thinly across both.

### What monthly budget do I need for Google Ads vs Meta Ads?
For Google Ads Search, we recommend a minimum of €1,500 per month for most EU service markets. Below this, you will not gather enough conversion data to optimise. For Meta Ads, €800-€1,200 per month is a viable starting point. Running both together effectively typically requires €3,000+ per month total.

### Can I run both platforms with the same ad creative?
No — and this is a common mistake. Google Search ads are text-driven and should match specific keyword intent. Meta ads need strong visuals and interruptive messaging to stop the scroll. The creative approach, copy style, and call-to-action should be tailored to each platform. A testimonial video works brilliantly on Meta. A clear headline with a price or offer works better on Google Search.
