# AI Overviews in Google: How to Show Up and What It Means for SEO

Google's AI Overviews — formerly Search Generative Experience (SGE) — represent the most significant change to search results in a decade. These AI-generated summaries appear at the top of select search results, providing direct answers synthesised from multiple sources. For service businesses, understanding **ai overviews seo** is critical to maintaining visibility.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **AI Overviews appear for 15–20% of queries** in participating markets, with expansion ongoing.
> - **Informational queries are most affected** — transactional and local queries less so.
> - **Structured content, FAQ schema, and E-E-A-T** are the primary optimisation levers.
> - **Zero-click searches will increase** for simple queries, but complex service queries still drive clicks.
> - **EU rollout is gradual** — Germany and France have partial availability as of early 2026.

## What Are Google's AI Overviews?

**AI Overviews** are AI-generated summaries that appear above traditional search results for certain queries. They synthesise information from multiple web sources to provide a direct answer, with links to source websites.

**Example:** A search for "what is Google Ads Performance Max" might display an AI Overview explaining the concept, with expandable sections and links to 3–5 source websites.

## How to Optimise for AI Overviews

### 1. Target Question-Based Queries

AI Overviews appear most frequently for:
- "What is..." queries
- "How to..." queries
- "Why does..." queries
- Comparison queries ("vs" searches)
- Definition queries

### 2. Use FAQ Schema Markup

FAQ schema is one of the strongest signals for AI Overview inclusion. Mark up common questions and concise answers on your service pages.

### 3. Provide Clear, Concise Definitions

AI Overviews pull definitions verbatim. Format your definitions clearly:

**Format:** "[Term] is [1-sentence definition]. [1-2 sentences of expansion]."

### 4. Structure Content for Synthesis

Use clear headings, bullet points, and tables. AI models synthesise structured content more easily than dense prose.

### 5. Build E-E-A-T Signals

AI Overviews prioritise content from authoritative sources. Strengthen your:
- Author expertise and credentials
- Backlink profile
- Brand mentions
- Review signals

## Impact on SEO and Traffic

### Zero-Click Searches Will Increase

For simple informational queries, users may get their answer from the AI Overview without clicking. Expect 10–20% traffic reduction for purely informational content.

### Complex Queries Still Drive Clicks

Service-related queries — "hire marketing agency Berlin," "best tax consultant for startups" — require evaluation and contact. These will continue driving clicks.

### Brand Search Becomes More Valuable

Users who see your brand in an AI Overview may search for you directly later. Track branded search volume as a key metric.

## FAQ

### What are Google AI Overviews?
AI Overviews are AI-generated summaries that appear at the top of Google search results for certain queries, synthesising information from multiple web sources into a direct answer.

### How do I get my content in AI Overviews?
Create clear, authoritative, well-structured content that directly answers common questions. Use FAQ schema, provide concise definitions, and build E-E-A-T signals.

### Will AI Overviews kill SEO?
No. SEO evolves. AI Overviews change which content gets clicked, but they do not eliminate the need for high-quality, authoritative content. Complex service queries will continue driving clicks.

### Do AI Overviews appear for all searches?
No. Currently 15–20% of queries in participating markets. Expansion is ongoing but gradual, especially in the EU where regulatory review is stricter.

### How do I measure AI Overview impact?
Track: branded search volume, featured snippet appearances, click-through rate trends, and direct traffic. Google Search Console may add AI Overview-specific data in the future.
