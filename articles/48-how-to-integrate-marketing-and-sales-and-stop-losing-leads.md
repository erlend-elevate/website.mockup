# How to Integrate Marketing and Sales (and Stop Losing Leads)

Marketing generates leads. Sales closes deals. In between, 50–80% of leads die — ignored, mishandled, or forgotten. This gap between **marketing and sales alignment** is the single biggest leak in most service business pipelines.

This guide covers why leads fall through the cracks, how to fix the handoff process, and how to align both teams around shared goals.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **50–80% of marketing leads never receive a sales follow-up.**
> - **A Service Level Agreement (SLA)** between marketing and sales eliminates ambiguity.
> - **Lead scoring** ensures sales only receives qualified, sales-ready leads.
> - **CRM integration** with marketing automation closes the visibility gap.
> - **Shared dashboards** create accountability for both teams.

## Why Leads Get Lost

| Problem | Cause | Impact |
|---------|-------|--------|
| No follow-up | Sales does not know lead exists | 50%+ of leads ignored |
| Slow response | No SLA for lead response time | Lead goes cold |
| Poor handoff | Marketing provides minimal context | Sales has no conversation starter |
| Mismatched definitions | "Lead" means different things | Sales rejects marketing leads |
| No feedback loop | Sales does not report lead quality | Marketing optimises for wrong metrics |
| Different tools | Marketing and sales use separate systems | No shared visibility |

## The Lead Handoff Process

| Stage | Marketing Responsibility | Sales Responsibility | Timeline |
|-------|-------------------------|---------------------|----------|
| Capture | Create lead magnet, run ads, capture form | — | Immediate |
| Qualify | Score leads (demographic + behavioural) | — | Real-time |
| Nurture | Email sequence, content delivery | — | 7–21 days |
| MQL → SQL | Mark as sales-qualified when score threshold met | Review and accept/reject | Within 24h |
| Contact | Provide lead context, history, interests | Contact within SLA | <2 hours |
| Close | Support with content, case studies | Discovery call, proposal, close | Per sales cycle |
| Feedback | Receive lead quality feedback | Report lead quality monthly | Monthly |

## Building the SLA

A Service Level Agreement between marketing and sales defines:

| Element | Marketing Commitment | Sales Commitment |
|---------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Lead volume | 50 MQLs per month | Review all MQLs within 24h |
| Lead quality | 30% SQL conversion rate | Accept/reject with reason |
| Response time | — | Contact SQLs within 2 hours |
| Attempts | — | 5 contact attempts over 14 days |
| Feedback | Receive monthly quality scores | Submit monthly quality feedback |
| Shared goals | Revenue target: €X | Revenue target: €X |

## FAQ

### What is marketing and sales alignment?
The process of ensuring marketing and sales teams work toward shared goals with clear handoff processes, shared definitions, and mutual accountability.

### How quickly should sales follow up on leads?
Within 2 hours for hot leads (direct enquiries). Within 24 hours for warm leads (content downloads, webinar attendees). Leads contacted within 5 minutes are 21x more likely to convert.

### What is a good lead-to-customer conversion rate?
For service businesses: 2–5% of marketing leads become customers. 15–25% of sales-qualified leads close. Track both metrics separately.

### How do I get sales to give feedback on lead quality?
Make it easy. Include a 2-question feedback form in your CRM (quality score 1–5, reason). Review monthly in a joint meeting. Incentivise feedback with shared bonus structures.

### What tool connects marketing and sales?
A CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce) that integrates with your marketing automation platform. Shared dashboards in Looker Studio or the CRM's native reporting.
