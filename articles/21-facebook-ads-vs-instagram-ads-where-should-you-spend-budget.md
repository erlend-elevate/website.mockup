# Facebook Ads vs Instagram Ads: Where Should You Spend Budget?

Meta's advertising ecosystem spans two of the world's largest social platforms. Both use the same Ads Manager, the same targeting, and the same billing. But the audiences, behaviours, and optimal creative formats differ significantly. Choosing the right platform — or the right split — can reduce your cost per lead by 30–50%.

This guide compares **facebook vs instagram ads** across demographics, costs, formats, and performance to help you allocate budget intelligently.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **Facebook reaches broader demographics** including 35–65+ age groups. Instagram skews younger (18–34).
> - **Facebook performs better for B2B services** and lead generation. Instagram excels for visual, lifestyle services.
> - **Instagram CPC is typically 20–40% higher** but engagement rates are 2–3x higher.
> - **Most service businesses should test both** and let performance data drive the split.
> - **Reels placements are the fastest-growing ad inventory** on both platforms.

## When to Use Facebook

**B2B service businesses.** Facebook's older demographic includes decision-makers, managers, and business owners. Lead forms and Messenger ads perform better on Facebook for professional services.

**Local service businesses.** Facebook Marketplace and local awareness campaigns drive foot traffic and local enquiries effectively.

**High-ticket services.** The 35–55 demographic on Facebook has higher purchasing power and is more likely to engage with complex offers.

**Retargeting.** Facebook's broader user base creates larger custom audiences for retargeting campaigns.

## When to Use Instagram

**Visual and lifestyle services.** Interior design, architecture, cosmetic clinics, fitness, photography, and premium hospitality thrive on Instagram's image-first format.

**Younger demographics.** If your target audience is under 35, Instagram is essential. Only 15% of Instagram users are over 45.

**Brand awareness.** Instagram's engagement rates are 2–3x higher than Facebook, making it ideal for top-of-funnel awareness campaigns.

**Reels and Stories.** Instagram users consume Reels and Stories at higher rates than Facebook users. Short-form video ads perform exceptionally here.

## Testing Strategy

Start with a 50/50 split. Run identical creative on both platforms for 2–3 weeks. Measure:
- Cost per click
- Cost per lead
- Lead quality (from CRM)
- Engagement rate
- Conversion rate

Then reallocate budget to the better-performing platform. Review monthly — platform performance shifts over time.

## FAQ

### Which is cheaper, Facebook or Instagram ads?
Facebook typically has 20–40% lower CPC. However, Instagram often delivers higher engagement and better brand recall. Calculate cost per qualified lead, not just CPC.

### Should I run the same ads on both platforms?
You can use the same creative, but optimise formatting. Instagram requires square (1:1) or vertical (4:5, 9:16) formats. Facebook accommodates more horizontal layouts. Test platform-specific creative.

### Do Instagram ads work for B2B?
For certain B2B segments — creative industries, tech startups, design services — yes. For traditional B2B (manufacturing, logistics, professional services), Facebook or LinkedIn typically outperform Instagram.

### What is the best ad format for service businesses?
Video testimonials and case study carousels consistently outperform static images for service businesses. On Instagram, Reels ads are the fastest-growing and often most cost-effective format.

### How do I track ROI across both platforms?
Use Meta's Ads Manager combined with UTM parameters and GA4. Import offline conversion data from your CRM to attribute actual revenue to each platform. Avoid relying solely on platform-reported metrics.
