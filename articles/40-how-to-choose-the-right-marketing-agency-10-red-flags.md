# How to Choose the Right Marketing Agency (10 Red Flags)

Choosing a marketing agency is one of the most consequential decisions a service business makes. The right partner accelerates growth. The wrong one burns budget, wastes months, and leaves you worse off than when you started. This guide presents 10 red flags to watch for when you **choose marketing agency**, plus the green flags that signal a trustworthy partner.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **No case studies or references** means no proven track record. Move on.
> - **Guaranteed rankings or results** are impossible promises made by dishonest agencies.
> - **No reporting or transparency** means they have something to hide.
> - **The cheapest option** is never the best — you get what you pay for.
> - **Ask about their EU/GDPR experience** — non-EU agencies often lack this expertise.

## The 10 Red Flags

### Red Flag 1: No Case Studies or Client References

An agency without documented results has nothing to show for their work. Case studies should include specific metrics, client names (with permission), and before/after comparisons.

**Green flag:** Detailed case studies with quantified results across multiple industries.

### Red Flag 2: Guaranteed Rankings or Results

"We guarantee #1 on Google" or " Guaranteed 10x ROAS" are lies. No ethical agency guarantees specific results because too many variables are outside their control.

**Green flag:** Realistic projections based on data, with clear caveats about what results depend on.

### Red Flag 3: No Reporting or Transparency

If an agency cannot explain what they are doing, why they are doing it, and what results it produced, they are either incompetent or hiding poor performance.

**Green flag:** Weekly or monthly reports with clear metrics, context, and next steps. Full access to your ad accounts and analytics.

### Red Flag 4: Cheapest Option Wins

Budget agencies cut corners: template strategies, junior staff, minimal reporting. The money you "save" is lost in poor performance.

**Green flag:** Transparent pricing with clear deliverables. Mid-range pricing with strong case studies beats cheap with no proof.

### Red Flag 5: No Industry Experience

An agency that only works with e-commerce may not understand B2B service sales cycles, lead quality, or professional services marketing.

**Green flag:** Experience in your industry or closely related verticals. Ask for specific examples.

### Red Flag 6: Hidden Fees and Lock-In Contracts

"Oh, that is not included" and 12-month contracts with no exit clause are predatory practices.

**Green flag:** Clear scope of work with all costs documented. Short initial contracts (1–3 months) with performance clauses.

### Red Flag 7: No Strategy Session

An agency that pitches without understanding your business, goals, and challenges is selling a template, not a solution.

**Green flag:** A thorough discovery process: your business, your market, your goals, your constraints. A customised proposal, not a generic pitch deck.

### Red Flag 8: Pushy Sales Tactics

High-pressure closes, limited-time discounts, and "sign today or the price goes up" are warning signs.

**Green flag:** Consultative approach. They want to ensure fit as much as you do. No pressure to decide immediately.

### Red Flag 9: No References Available

An agency unwilling to connect you with current or past clients has something to hide.

**Green flag:** Willing introduction to 2–3 current clients who can speak to their experience.

### Red Flag 10: One-Size-Fits-All Approach

"We use the same strategy for everyone" means your unique challenges are ignored.

**Green flag:** Customised strategy based on your specific situation, goals, and market.

## Questions to Ask During Agency Interviews

1. "Can you share 3 case studies from businesses like mine?"
2. "What is your reporting process and frequency?"
3. "Who will actually work on my account?"
4. "What happens if results do not meet projections?"
5. "How do you handle GDPR and EU compliance?"
6. "What is your contract structure and exit clause?"
7. "How do you measure success?"
8. "What channels do you recommend and why?"
9. "How do you stay current with platform changes?"
10. "Can I speak to a current client?"

## FAQ

### What is the most important factor when choosing a marketing agency?
Proven results in your industry with transparent reporting. Everything else — pricing, personality, pitch quality — is secondary to demonstrated performance.

### Should I choose a specialist or full-service agency?
For service businesses, specialist agencies (PPC, SEO, email) typically deliver better results than full-service agencies that spread expertise thin. Coordinate multiple specialists or choose a full-service agency with deep expertise in your priority channels.

### How long should an agency contract be?
Start with 1–3 months. Extend based on results. Avoid 12-month contracts without performance clauses. Performance-based pricing eliminates contract length concerns.

### What should I expect in the first 30 days?
Account audit, strategy development, tracking setup, and initial campaign launches. Meaningful results typically appear in 60–90 days. Be wary of agencies promising instant results.

### How much should I pay a marketing agency?
For small service businesses: €1,500–€5,000/month. For mid-size: €5,000–€15,000/month. For large: €15,000–€50,000+/month. Add ad spend on top of management fees.
