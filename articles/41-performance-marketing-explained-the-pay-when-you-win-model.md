# Performance Marketing Explained: The Pay-When-You-Win Model

Most marketing agencies charge monthly retainers regardless of results. You pay €3,000, €5,000, €10,000 per month — and hope something works. **Performance marketing** flips this model. You pay only when results are delivered: a lead generated, a sale made, a target hit.

This guide explains how performance-based marketing works, when it makes sense, and how Elevate Marketing's model delivers value without the risk of traditional retainers.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **Performance marketing means paying for results, not hours.** The agency bears the risk.
> - **It aligns incentives** — the agency only wins when you win.
> - **It works best for businesses with clear conversion events** — form fills, calls, bookings, sales.
> - **Not every service suits performance-based pricing.** Long, complex sales cycles are harder to structure.
> - **Transparency and tracking** are essential for performance marketing to work.

## What Is Performance Marketing?

**Performance marketing** is a results-based pricing model where the marketing provider is compensated based on achieving specific, measurable outcomes — not based on time spent or activities performed.

**Common performance models:**

| Model | How It Works | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Pay per lead | Fixed fee per qualified lead | Lead generation businesses |
| Pay per sale | Commission on closed revenue | E-commerce, productised services |
| Pay per booking | Fee per confirmed appointment | Clinics, consultancies |
| Pay per acquisition | Fixed fee per new customer | Subscription businesses |
| Revenue share | Percentage of revenue generated | High-LTV services |
| Hybrid | Base fee + performance bonus | Complex B2B services |

## Performance Marketing vs. Traditional Retainer

| Factor | Traditional Retainer | Performance Marketing |
|--------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Pricing basis | Time + activities | Results delivered |
| Risk | Client bears all risk | Shared or agency-biased |
| Incentives | Bill more hours | Deliver more results |
| Transparency | Variable | High — results are measurable |
| Cash flow | Predictable for agency | Variable for agency |
| Client commitment | Monthly regardless | Pay only for success |
| Set-up time | Immediate | Longer (tracking, alignment) |
| Best for | Brand building, complex strategy | Lead gen, direct response |

## When Performance Marketing Works

**It works when:**
- You have clear, trackable conversion events
- Your customer lifetime value exceeds €500
- Your sales cycle is under 90 days
- You have reliable tracking and attribution
- Your market has sufficient search volume or audience size
- You are willing to share data and collaborate

**It does not work when:**
- Your sales cycle is 6+ months with many offline touchpoints
- Your average deal value is under €300
- You cannot track conversions accurately
- Your market is extremely niche (<1,000 prospects)
- You are not willing to share CRM data or feedback

## FAQ

### What is performance marketing?
Performance marketing is a pricing model where you pay marketing providers based on achieved results (leads, sales, bookings) rather than time or activities.

### Is performance marketing more expensive?
Per-lead costs are often higher than retainer-model CPL, but total cost is lower because you only pay for results. The agency bears the risk of poor performance.

### How do you define a "qualified lead"?
We agree on lead criteria upfront: job title, company size, service interest, budget range, and geography. Only leads meeting all criteria are billable.

### What if I do not get enough leads?
Performance marketing includes minimum volume commitments. If we cannot deliver agreed volumes, you can terminate without penalty.

### Do you still charge a setup fee?
Some performance arrangements include a nominal setup fee (€500–€2,000) to cover initial tracking, landing page creation, and campaign build. Pure performance (no setup fee) is available for larger engagements.
