# A/B Testing for Beginners: What, Why, and How

**A/B testing** is the practice of comparing two versions of something — a webpage, email, ad — to determine which performs better. It removes guesswork from marketing decisions and replaces opinions with data. For service businesses, it is the most reliable way to improve conversion rates without increasing traffic costs.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **A/B testing compares two versions** of a single element to find what converts better.
> - **Test one variable at a time** — otherwise you cannot know what caused the difference.
> - **Statistical significance requires 100+ conversions per variant.** Smaller samples produce unreliable results.
> - **Headlines, CTAs, and form length** typically produce the biggest conversion improvements.
> - **Free tools** like Google Optimize (sunsetting) alternatives make A/B testing accessible to all businesses.

## What Is A/B Testing?

**A/B testing** (or split testing) splits your audience into two groups:
- **Group A** sees the original version (control)
- **Group B** sees the modified version (variant)

You measure which version achieves your goal (conversion, click, form fill) more often. The version with the statistically significant higher conversion rate wins.

## What to Test

| Element | Example A | Example B | Expected Impact |
|---------|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| Headline | "Book a Free Consultation" | "Get Your Free Strategy Call" | 20–40% |
| CTA button | "Submit" | "Get My Free Quote" | 10–25% |
| Button colour | Blue | Orange/Green | 5–15% |
| Form length | 6 fields | 3 fields | 15–30% |
| Hero image | Team photo | Client result | 10–20% |
| Social proof placement | Bottom of page | Below headline | 10–20% |
| Pricing display | "From €500" | "€500/month" | 5–15% |
| Testimonials | Text only | Video | 15–25% |
| Page layout | Long-form | Short-form | 10–20% |
| Trust badges | None | Security + review badges | 5–15% |

## How to Run an A/B Test

### Step 1: Identify What to Test

Use the ICE scoring framework:

| Criteria | Score (1–10) | Weight |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Impact | How much could this improve conversion? | High |
| Confidence | How sure are you this matters? | Medium |
| Ease | How easy is it to implement? | Medium |

Priority = Impact × Confidence × Ease. Test highest scores first.

### Step 2: Create Your Hypothesis

"If we [change], then [metric] will [increase/decrease] because [reason]."

Example: "If we reduce the form from 6 fields to 3, then form completion rate will increase 20% because mobile users abandon long forms."

### Step 3: Set Up the Test

Use an A/B testing tool (see below). Split traffic 50/50. Ensure the test runs until statistical significance is reached.

### Step 4: Run and Monitor

Minimum test duration: 2 weeks. Required conversions per variant: 100+. Check for external factors (seasonality, promotions) that could skew results.

### Step 5: Analyse and Implement

If the variant wins with 95%+ statistical confidence, implement it. If inconclusive, test a different variable.

## Statistical Significance

| Conversions per Variant | Confidence Level | Reliable? |
|------------------------|-----------------|-----------|
| <50 | Low | No |
| 50–100 | Medium | Maybe |
| 100–200 | High | Yes |
| 200+ | Very High | Yes |

**Rule of thumb:** Run tests for at least 2 weeks and until each variant reaches 100+ conversions.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Fails | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Testing multiple variables | Cannot isolate what changed | Test one variable at a time |
| Insufficient sample size | Results are random noise | Wait for 100+ conversions |
| Too short duration | External factors skew results | Minimum 2 weeks |
| Not testing at all | Decisions based on opinions | Start with headline tests |
| Testing tiny changes | No meaningful impact | Test elements with high impact |
| Ignoring statistical significance | False positives | Wait for 95% confidence |

## FAQ

### What is A/B testing in simple terms?
Comparing two versions of something (webpage, email, ad) to see which performs better. Half your audience sees version A, half sees version B. The winner is the one with more conversions.

### How long should an A/B test run?
Minimum 2 weeks. Until you reach 100+ conversions per variant. Through complete business cycles (weekends, weekdays).

### What should I test first?
Headlines. They have the highest impact on conversion and are the easiest to change. Test CTA buttons second, then form length.

### Is A/B testing worth it for small businesses?
Yes. Even a 10% conversion improvement on a landing page can double your lead volume without spending more on ads. Small improvements compound over time.

### What happened to Google Optimize?
Google Optimize was discontinued in September 2023. Alternatives: VWO, Unbounce, Convert, and Optimizely offer similar functionality.
