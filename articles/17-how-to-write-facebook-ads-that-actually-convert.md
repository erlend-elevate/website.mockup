# How to Write Facebook Ads That Actually Convert

Writing **facebook ad copy** that converts is not about clever wordplay. It is about understanding your prospect's problem, agitating it, presenting your solution, and making the next step obvious. Most service businesses fail at this because they write ads about themselves, not about the customer.

This guide covers four proven copy formulas, headline frameworks that stop the scroll, body copy structure, CTA variations, and creative best practices specifically for EU service businesses. Every example is grounded in real-world application, not marketing theory.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve)** is the most reliable formula for service business ads.
> - **Your headline has 3 seconds** to earn a scroll-stop. Lead with the problem or the outcome.
> - **Body copy should read like a conversation**, not a brochure. Short sentences. Active voice.
> - **One CTA per ad.** Multiple calls-to-action dilute response and confuse the algorithm.
> - **Video testimonials outperform static images** by 30–50% for service businesses.

## The Four Copy Formulas That Work

### 1. PAS: Problem-Agitate-Solve

**Structure:** Identify the problem → Amplify the pain → Present your solution.

**Example for a business consultant:**
> *"70% of small manufacturing firms in Bavaria lose money on operational inefficiencies they cannot see. (Problem) Every month you delay, you're bleeding margin on wasted hours, excess inventory, and missed deadlines. (Agitate) We identify and fix these leaks in 90 days — guaranteed. Book your free operational audit. (Solve)"

PAS works because it mirrors how people actually make decisions: they feel pain, they want relief, they need a specific path forward.

### 2. AIDA: Attention-Interest-Desire-Action

**Structure:** Grab attention → Build interest → Create desire → Demand action.

**Example for a dental clinic:**
> *"Smile makeover in 48 hours — without the London price tag. (Attention) Our Lisbon clinic uses the same porcelain veneers as Harley Street practices, at 40% less. (Interest) Imagine walking into your next meeting with complete confidence. (Desire) Limited consultations this month. Book yours now. (Action)"

### 3. Storytelling: Before-After-Bridge

**Structure:** Show the before state → Show the after state → Bridge with your method.

**Example for a tax consultant:**
> *"Last January, a Berlin restaurant owner came to us owing €47,000 in back taxes. (Before) By March, we had reduced that liability to €12,000 through legitimate structuring. (After) Here's how we did it — and how we can do the same for your business. (Bridge)"

### 4. Social Proof + Offer

**Structure:** Lead with proof → Follow with a specific, time-bound offer.

**Example for a marketing agency:**
> *"'We generated 340 qualified leads in 60 days.' — Maria K., CFO, Stuttgart Manufacturing. (Proof) Our performance-based model means you pay only for results, not hours. (Offer) 3 client slots open for Q2. Apply for a free strategy call."

## Creative Best Practices for Service Ads

**Video vs. Image:** Video ads typically generate 30–50% more engagement for service businesses. A 30–60 second client testimonial or service explainer outperforms static imagery.

**Carousel format:** Use carousels to tell a multi-step story. Card 1: Problem. Card 2: Process. Card 3: Results. Card 4: CTA.

**User-generated content:** Authentic photos and videos from real clients outperform polished studio content. Encourage clients to share their experience.

## GDPR Compliance for Meta Ads in the EU

- **Consent:** Explicit opt-in required before tracking. Use a Consent Management Platform.
- **Transparency:** Clear privacy policy linked from landing pages.
- **Data minimisation:** Only collect information necessary for the service.
- **Right to erasure:** Honour data deletion requests within 30 days.
- **Special categories:** Avoid targeting based on health, religion, or political affiliation unless explicitly permitted.

## FAQ

### What is the most effective Facebook ad formula for services?
PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve) consistently outperforms for service businesses because it aligns with how people make purchase decisions: identifying pain, amplifying urgency, and presenting a clear solution.

### How long should Facebook ad copy be?
For cold audiences, 50–125 words. For warm audiences (retargeting), 20–50 words. Lead with the hook in the first 15 words.

### Should I use emojis in Facebook ads?
Sparingly. One or two relevant emojis can increase engagement by 15–20%, but overuse looks unprofessional — especially for B2B services.

### How many ads should I test at once?
Test 2–3 variations per ad set. Change one variable at a time: headline, image, or CTA. Run until you reach 100 clicks per variant.

### What CTA works best for service businesses?
"Book a free consultation" and "Get a free quote" consistently outperform generic CTAs like "Learn more." The word "free" reduces friction significantly.
