# GEO Explained: How to Get Your Business Found by ChatGPT

Every day, millions of people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude for recommendations. "Find me a good tax consultant in Lisbon." "Which digital marketing agency specialises in B2B services?" "Recommend a GDPR compliance consultant for healthcare." If your business is not showing up in these AI-generated answers, you are missing a fast-growing source of qualified leads.

**GEO — Generative Engine Optimisation** — is the practice of optimising your online presence so AI search engines and large language models (LLMs) discover, trust, and cite your business. Unlike traditional SEO, which targets Google's algorithmic ranking, **geo optimisation** targets how AI models select and present information.

> **TL;DR — Key Takeaways**
> - **GEO is the new SEO** — optimising for AI citations, not just Google rankings.
> - **AI models prioritise clear, factual, well-structured content** from authoritative sources.
> - **Structured data, brand mentions, and FAQ-style content** increase citation likelihood.
> - **Your Google Business Profile and reviews** heavily influence AI local recommendations.
> - **Consistency across the web** is critical — AI models cross-reference multiple sources.

## What Is GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation)?

**GEO optimisation** is the discipline of making your business discoverable and citable by AI-powered search engines and conversational AI platforms. It combines traditional SEO, structured data, content clarity, and brand authority signals to increase the probability that LLMs recommend your business.

## GEO vs SEO: Key Differences

| Factor | Traditional SEO | GEO (AI Search Optimisation) |
|--------|----------------|------------------------------|
| Target | Google's ranking algorithm | LLM citation and recommendation |
| Goal | Rank in SERPs | Be cited in AI-generated answers |
| Content focus | Keywords + backlinks | Clarity + structure + authority |
| Success metric | Ranking position, organic traffic | Brand mentions in AI responses |
| Optimisation | On-page + off-page + technical | Structured data + brand signals + content clarity |
| Result format | Blue link | Direct answer / recommendation |

## How AI Models Choose What to Cite

LLMs like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude use several signals to select sources:

1. **Authority and trust:** Content from established, credible sources
2. **Clarity and structure:** Well-organised, clearly written content
3. **Recency and freshness:** Up-to-date information
4. **Citation density:** Content that is frequently cited by other sources
5. **Structured data:** Schema markup that machines can parse easily
6. **Consistency:** Business information consistent across the web

## GEO Strategies for Service Businesses

### 1. Structure Content for AI Consumption

AI models prefer content that follows clear patterns:
- **Define terms early:** "[Service] is [definition]"
- **Use structured lists:** Bulleted and numbered lists are easier to parse
- **Include FAQ sections:** Direct questions with concise answers
- **Provide comparison tables:** Structured comparisons are highly citable

### 2. Implement Comprehensive Schema Markup

| Schema Type | Purpose | Priority |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| Organization | Brand identity and contact info | Critical |
| LocalBusiness | Geographic and service data | Critical |
| Service | Specific service descriptions | High |
| FAQPage | Question-answer pairs | High |
| HowTo | Process-based content | Medium |
| Review | Customer feedback aggregation | Medium |

### 3. Build Brand Mentions Across the Web

AI models scan the entire web for brand mentions. The more credible sources mention your business, the more likely AI is to recommend you.

- Industry directories and listings
- Guest articles on respected publications
- Podcast appearances
- Awards and recognitions
- Professional association memberships

### 4. Optimise Your Google Business Profile

For local service queries, AI models heavily weight Google Business Profile data. Ensure yours is complete, accurate, and actively managed.

### 5. Create Citable, Answer-First Content

Every piece of content should answer a question clearly and early. The first paragraph should provide the answer; subsequent paragraphs provide context and detail.

**Example:**
> *"A tax consultant in Portugal typically charges between €75 and €250 per hour, depending on specialisation and experience. Here's how pricing breaks down by service type..."*

## FAQ

### What is GEO in marketing?
GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) is the practice of optimising your online presence so AI search engines and large language models discover, trust, and cite your business in their responses.

### How is GEO different from SEO?
SEO targets Google's ranking algorithm to appear in search results. GEO targets AI models to be cited in conversational responses. They overlap significantly but differ in emphasis — GEO prioritises content clarity, structured data, and brand authority signals.

### Can I optimise specifically for ChatGPT?
Not directly — ChatGPT's training data and response generation are not publicly manipulable. However, by following GEO best practices (structured data, clear content, brand mentions), you increase your probability of being cited.

### Does GEO replace SEO?
No. GEO complements SEO. Most GEO tactics (structured data, quality content, brand authority) also improve traditional SEO. Invest in both simultaneously.

### How do I measure GEO success?
Track: brand mentions in AI responses (manual testing), increases in branded search volume, citation in featured snippets, and growth in direct traffic.
