About JLM

Jepson Longstaff Midgley Solicitors LLP has been based in Wykeham since the firm was founded in 2012. We are a small practice by design: three solicitors, handling work personally from start to finish.

Our clients are individuals, families and owner-managed businesses across Scarborough, the Yorkshire coast and the North York Moors. Much of our work comes from word of mouth and from clients returning to us over the years.

We are authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA number 567609) and registered with Companies House as OC375646.
