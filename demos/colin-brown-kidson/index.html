About CBK

Colin Brown & Kidson has served Whitby and the surrounding coast since 1914. Over four generations, our role has stayed the same: give people honest, understandable legal advice, and get the work done without fuss.

We are a small, approachable team. When you instruct us, you deal directly with a qualified solicitor — not a call centre. Most of our work comes from clients returning to us, or from their families and neighbours.

We are authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA number 44283), and hold the following professional accreditations:

Lexcel accredited Conveyancing Quality Scheme (CQS) Resolution member
