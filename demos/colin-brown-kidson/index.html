About the Firm

One practice. One town. Four generations.

Colin Brown & Kidson has served Whitby and the surrounding coast since 1914. Over four generations our role has stayed the same: give people honest, understandable advice, and get the work done without fuss.

We are a small team by choice. When you instruct us, you deal directly with a qualified solicitor — not a call centre, not a case handler. Most of our work comes from clients returning to us, or from their families and neighbours recommending us.

Our office sits on Wellington Road, a short walk from the harbour. We see clients face-to-face whenever it helps, but we’re equally set up to advise over the phone or by email for those who prefer it.
